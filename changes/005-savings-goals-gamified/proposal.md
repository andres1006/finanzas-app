# Proposal: Gamified Savings Goals & Detailed Tracking

## Goal
Transform the Savings Goals module into a motivating, gamified experience with milestones (milestones/awards), detailed contribution history, and real-time progress updates.

## Scope
- **Contribution System**: Ability to add specific amounts to a goal with date and notes.
- **Detailed History**: A per-goal view showing every "agregación" with its date.
- **Gamification (Milestones)**: Implementation of "Medals" or "Awards" when reaching specific thresholds (e.g., 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% of the goal).
- **Dynamic Calculation**: Automatic recalculation of percentage and remaining amount.

## Context
Current goals are static. We need to track the "flow" of money into these goals and celebrate progress to keep the users motivated.
